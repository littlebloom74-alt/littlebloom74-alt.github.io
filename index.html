Pıtırcık ve Ormandaki Sırlar

A short animated series for children aged 4–8, published on the Little Bloom YouTube channel.

Every episode is a 20-second story about Pıtırcık the squirrel, Hüsnü the wise owl and Pofuduk the rabbit, each teaching a small value such as sharing, courage, friendship and caring for nature.

About "Pitircik Otomasyon"

Pitircik Otomasyon is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Little Bloom channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and helps prepare each episode (script, illustration, voiceover and short animation). The finished video is e-mailed to the owner for review, and only after the owner manually approves it is the video uploaded to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.
